# Sprint Forecast & Capacity Planning

## 1. Sprint Parameters
- **Sprint Duration:** 1 week per sprint.
- **Team Size:** 4 to 5 members.
- **Target Velocity:** Estimated at **10 to 12 story points per sprint**, accounting for lab schedules, code reviews, and test writing constraints.

## 2. Release Roadmap & Timeline
- **MVP Scope (~35 points):** 
  - Scheduled across **Sprints 1 & 2** (approx. 2 weeks of development) to ensure the core menu, cart, slot capacity rules, and kitchen status board are fully functional and tested.
- **Full Backlog Completion (~50 points including stretch goals):** 
  - Projected to be completed across **Sprints 3 & 4**, leaving room for user feedback adjustments, bug fixes, and stretch features (like loyalty points and daily specials).
